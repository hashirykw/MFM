Syllabus map · the Seerah and the Caliphs

From Makka to Kufa. Seven eras. One story.

Sir Fazil teaches the history in date order, so every event meets its cause and its result. Drag through the eras the way the course runs, from 570 to 661.

01570–610

Before the call

Born in Makka in the Year of the Elephant and orphaned young, raised by Abd al-Muttalib, then Abu Talib. Known as al-Amin; married Khadijah in 595 and settled the Black Stone dispute in 605.

570576595605
02610–622

Makka: the message

The first revelation in the Cave of Hira, then open preaching and persecution. The migration to Abyssinia, the boycott, the Year of Sorrow, Ta'if, and the Pledges of Aqaba.

610613615619
03622–624

Madina: the first community

The Hijra and Masjid Quba, then Masjid an-Nabawi. Brotherhood between the Ansar and the Muhajirun, the Constitution of Madina, and the change of qibla.

1 AHQubaAnsarQibla
04624–627

Badr, Uhud, the Trench

Victory at Badr in 2 AH, the lesson of Uhud in 3 AH, and the trench dug on Salman's advice in 5 AH, when the confederates' siege of Madina failed.

6246256275 AH
05628–632

Hudaybiyya to the Farewell

The Treaty of Hudaybiyya, letters to rulers, Khaybar, and Makka opened in 8 AH. The Farewell Pilgrimage and Sermon in 10 AH; the Prophet's death in 632.

628630631632
06632–644

Abu Bakr & Umar

The Ridda wars and the Qur'an gathered into one volume. Then Umar: the Hijri calendar, the diwan, Jerusalem, and an administration built to last.

632634636644
07644–661

Uthman & Ali

Standard copies of the Qur'an sent to the provinces, then Uthman's martyrdom in 656. Ali's caliphate, the Battle of the Camel and Siffin, until Kufa in 661.

Questions

Before you message.

still unsure? his team answers on WhatsApp

Should I take O Level 2058 or IGCSE 0493?

Your school or exam centre decides. Both have the same two papers and the same content; 2058 is graded A* to E and 0493 A* to G. The classes are the same.

Can I study online or from outside Karachi?

Yes. Live online batches run for students across Pakistan and abroad, and the recorded course on LearnedGuys covers every Paper 1 and Paper 2 topic in about 54 hours of Urdu lectures.

How do I register, and what are the fees?

Fill in the form below; WhatsApp opens with your details ready to send. His team confirms your batch and fees. Nothing is charged online.

How do I get an A* in Islamiyat?

Know the passages, Hadiths and history in order; back each point with a reference; and answer part (b) with a reason and an example from life today. Then practise topical past papers marked against the levels.

Who can use the AI checker and the portal?

Registered students. Your login arrives on WhatsApp once your batch is confirmed. Parents get a read-only view.

Is there a crash course before the exam?

Yes. The crash course covers the passages, Hadiths, the Seerah and full mocks in the last weeks before the exam; dates are announced on Instagram and the WhatsApp channel.
