f. 03 — What it covers

What a crash course focuses on.

01

Passages and Hadiths

All 15 Qur’anic passages and 20 Hadiths revised by theme, with the importance and the action Muslims take, so Question 1 of both papers is secure.

  • Themes in your own words
  • Importance today, with examples
  • Quick-fire recall drills
02

History in order

The Seerah, the first community, the history of the Qur’an and the Hadiths, and the four Caliphs, with the dates, names and numbers that lift part (a).

  • Timelines from 570 to 661
  • Battles and treaties in detail
  • Compilation, step by step
03

Timed papers and feedback

Full papers on the clock, marked against the levels, and the switch from part (a) knowledge to part (b) understanding drilled until it is automatic.

  • Two full mock papers
  • Marked answers with comments
  • Last-week exam tips
