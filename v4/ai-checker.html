f. 03 — A marked answer

A marked answer, start to finish.

A Paper 1, Question 2 answer on the history of the Qur’an. Tap a highlighted line to read the comment.

Paper 1 · Question 2(a) Write an account of how the Qur’an was compiled under Abu Bakr and Uthman. [10]
(b) Why was it important that the Qur’an was put into one book? [4]

(a) After the Prophet died, many Muslims who had memorised the Qur’an were martyred at the Battle of Yamama in 633. Umar was worried that parts of it would be lost, so he asked Abu Bakr to collect it in one place.

Abu Bakr gave the job to Zayd ibn Thabit, who collected it from many different places. The sheets were kept by Abu Bakr, then by Umar, and then by his daughter Hafsa.

Later, in Uthman’s time, Muslims in different places were reading the Qur’an differently. Uthman borrowed Hafsa’s copy, had copies made in the dialect of the Quraysh, sent them to the main cities and had the other copies burned.

(b) It was important because now everyone has the same Qur’an.

Part (b) · AO2 understanding2/4
  • The account is in the right order, from Yamama to Uthman’s copies.
  • Good detail on who kept the sheets: Abu Bakr, Umar, then Hafsa.
  • Name what Zayd collected from: parchment, bones, palm stalks and the memories of the Companions, each verse checked by two witnesses.
  • Say who raised the alarm in Uthman’s time: Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman, after campaigns in Armenia and Azerbaijan.
  • Part (b) needs a developed reason and an example from Muslim life today.

(b) A single agreed text kept the Ummah united: there could be no disputes over which words were the Qur’an. Today a Muslim in Karachi and a Muslim in Cairo recite exactly the same words in salat, and every hafiz learns the same text, so Allah’s promise to guard the Qur’an (Sura 15.9) is seen in practice.

A clear, well-ordered account. Add the materials and Hudhayfa and part (a) reaches Level 4. In part (b), always give one reason, explain it, and finish with today. 9 stands. — Sir Fazil
