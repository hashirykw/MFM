The teacher behind the top band.

Sir Mohammad Fazil Maniya teaches Cambridge O Level and IGCSE Islamiyat in Karachi and online. His students learn the passages, the Hadiths and the Seerah with the detail and references the mark scheme rewards, then learn to explain why each one matters to Muslims today.

A Cambridge-certified educationist with more than fifteen years in the classroom, he has mentored over seven thousand students. Born and raised in Karachi, he is a gold medalist in BSCS from IBA Karachi and holds Master's degrees in Computer Science and Islamic Studies. His aim is to revive the love of Allah, His Prophet ﷺ and Islam in young people.

What students get.

The same approach in Karachi, online and in the recorded course.

i.

The passages and Hadiths, secure

All 15 passages and 20 Hadiths, learned by theme and revised until they come out in the exam without a pause.

ii.

History in order

The Seerah and the Caliphs as one connected story, with the dates and numbers that lift a part (a) answer.

iii.

Evidence in every answer

The right verse or Hadith for each point, quoted briefly and accurately.

iv.

Part (b) that scores

Why it matters to Muslims today, explained with a real example, not another paragraph of history.

v.

Past papers by topic

Practised against the mark scheme, with exam technique drilled alongside the content.

vi.

Notes and a portal

Written notes for both papers, recorded lectures and the AI checker, all in one student login.
