Sir Fazil Maniya.

Sir Mohammad Fazil Maniya teaches Cambridge O Level and IGCSE Islamiyat in Karachi and online. His students learn the passages, the Hadiths and the Seerah with the detail and the references the mark scheme rewards, then learn to explain why each one matters to Muslims today.

A Cambridge-certified educationist with more than fifteen years in the classroom, he has mentored over seven thousand students. He is a gold medalist in BSCS from IBA Karachi and holds Master's degrees in Computer Science and Islamic Studies.
