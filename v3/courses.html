O Level Islamiyat 2058. Eight areas, two papers, graded A* to E.

Paper 1

The Qur'an, the Prophet and the first community

1h 30mduration
50marks
50%of the grade

Question 1: two of three set Qur'anic passages (8 marks). Question 2: the history and importance of the Qur'an (14). Then two of Questions 3–5 on the life of the Prophet and the first Islamic community (14 each).

1h 30mduration
50marks

Question 1: two of four set Hadiths (8 marks). Question 2: the history and importance of the Hadiths (14). Then two of Questions 3–5 on the Rightly Guided Caliphs and the Articles of Faith and Pillars of Islam (14 each).

Where the marks come from, in each paper

Knowledge · 34 marksUnderstanding · 16

Every 14-mark question is part (a), 10 marks for knowledge, and part (b), 4 marks for understanding. Question 1 is split 4 and 4.

Part (a) · 10

Knowledge

An accurate, detailed and well-ordered account, marked in levels.

Part (b) · 4

Understanding

Why it matters to Muslims, with a reason and an example from today.

Q1 · 8

Passages & Hadiths

Their themes or teachings, then their importance in Muslim life.

Every answer

Evidence

References to the Qur'an and Hadith lift answers into the top levels.

Six habits, every week. How the course runs.

01

In class or online

Classes at centres and schools across Karachi, live online for students outside the city, and a recorded course for self-paced study.

02

Passages and Hadiths weekly

A few passages and Hadiths every week, revised often, so all 35 are secure well before the exam.

03

Topical past papers

Past-paper questions by topic, marked against the mark scheme's levels.

04

Written notes

Notes for both papers, written the way 10-mark and 4-mark answers are rewarded.

05

Timelines and charts

The Seerah and the Caliphate in order, so dates and sequence are never a guess.

06

Exam technique

Command words, timing and the switch from part (a) to part (b), taught alongside the content.
