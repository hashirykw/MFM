How an answer earns its marks

Knowing the story is the first rung.

One Paper 1 question, four answers, each a rung higher. Pick a rung and watch the facts light up as the answer climbs.

Example · Paper 1 style

The Battle of Badr

Fought on 17 Ramadan, 2 AH near the wells of Badr. About 313 Muslims faced about 1,000 Makkans. Allah promised help with a thousand angels (8:9). Prisoners could earn their freedom by teaching ten Muslims to read. The lesson: faith and obedience count for more than numbers.

(a) Write an account of the Battle of Badr. [10]
(b) What can Muslims today learn from it? [4]
