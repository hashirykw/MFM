2058 — O Level

O Level Islamiyat

Eight areas across two papers: the Qur’an, the life of the Prophet and the first Muslims on Paper 1; the Hadiths, the Rightly Guided Caliphs and the beliefs and practices of Islam on Paper 2. Both papers are compulsory, graded A* to E in the June and November series.

Where the marks come from · 2058 and 0493

AO1 KnowledgeAO2 Understanding

Whole qualification. Every 14-mark question is part (a), 10 marks for knowledge, and part (b), 4 marks for understanding. Question 1 is split 4 and 4.

Part (a) → part (b)

Two kinds of marks.

Most lost marks come from answering part (b) with more history. Sir Fazil teaches the switch.

  • Part (a) · 10 marksKnowledge: an accurate, detailed and well-ordered account, marked in levels.
  • Part (b) · 4 marksUnderstanding: why it matters to Muslims, with a reason and an example from today.
  • Question 1 · 8 marksTwo passages or Hadiths: their themes or teachings, then their importance in Muslim life.
  • EvidenceReferences to the Qur’an and Hadith lift answers into the top levels.

How the course runs

Six habits, every week.

01

In class or online

Classes at academic centres and schools across Karachi, live online for students outside the city, and a recorded course for self-paced study.

02

Passages and Hadiths weekly

A few passages and Hadiths every week, revised often, so all 35 are secure well before the exam.

03

Topical past papers

Past-paper questions by topic, marked against the mark scheme’s levels.

04

Written notes

Notes for both papers, written the way 10-mark and 4-mark answers are rewarded.

05

Timelines and charts

The Seerah and the Caliphate in order, so dates and sequence are never a guess.

06

Exam technique

Command words, timing and the switch from part (a) to part (b), taught alongside the content rather than after it.
